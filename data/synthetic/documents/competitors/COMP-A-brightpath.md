# Brightpath Devices - competitor comparison

## Competitor snapshot

Brightpath Devices is a hardware-focused reseller. A customer reported that comparable hardware was roughly 8-10% below Cobalt Ridge pricing in 2026-09; this price claim is unverified. It is a hardware comparison, not a verified comparison of the full hardware, installation, and managed-service bundle.

## Service and delivery limits

Brightpath offers 9x5 business-hours support only. Its replacement model is return-to-depot, with a reported 5-7 business-day replacement window. Installation goes through an unnamed third-party subcontractor. Its current 6-8 week lead time puts a 2026-12-01 delivery at risk.

## How to position Cobalt Ridge

For 24x7 sites, explain the value of continuous remote monitoring, a 4-hour remote response target, and next-business-day advance hardware replacement. Cobalt Ridge certified field technicians, who are its own staff, perform installation. One seller owns the hardware, installation, and managed service, which limits handoffs when an always-on site needs help. Do not claim Brightpath cannot meet a particular customer's date without checking the actual order schedule.
