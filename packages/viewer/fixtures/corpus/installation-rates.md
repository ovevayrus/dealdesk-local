## Summary
Field engineering deployment rates and site installation labor specifications.

## Loaded Hourly Rate
Standard field technician labor is billed at a loaded internal cost of $125.00 per hour. Rack installation standards budget 32 labor hours per deployment cluster, resulting in a baseline installation cost of $4,000.00.

## Installation Scope
Scope includes site survey, physical rack anchoring, power distribution unit hookup, cable management, and initial network link validation.
