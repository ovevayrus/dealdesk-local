## Summary
Standard shipping lane schedules, freight commitments, and logistics rate cards.

## Lane Rates
Standard continental US freight lane rate is $1,000.00 per full truckload shipment. Two scheduled shipments for 100 rack units total $2,000.00 base freight expense under regular transit schedules.

## Stress Lane Surcharges
In high-demand periods or fuel shock scenarios, expedited freight lane surcharges increase shipment costs up to $1,700.00 per shipment ($3,400.00 for two shipments).
