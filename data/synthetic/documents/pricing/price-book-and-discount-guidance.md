# Cobalt Ridge price book and discount guidance

## Reference bundle

Bundle SYN-100-UNITS-12-MONTH-SERVICE has a list contract price of USD 160,000.00. It covers 100 CR-200 Edge Gateway units, installation, and the first 12 months of Cobalt Ridge Managed Edge Service for 100 active units. The reference payment schedule is standard_upfront. This is a list price, not a customer-specific approved offer.

## Economic gate

Synthetic policy synthetic-demo-v1 requires at least 25% gross margin against stress cost. An offer below that floor is blocked. The pricing engine calculates costs, the economic floor, and approval outcomes for the actual quote scope and dates. This guidance does not set a specific deal price.

## Discount authority

A representative may approve a discount through 10% inclusive when every other gate passes. A discount above 10% through 20% inclusive requires sales director approval before release. A discount above 20% is blocked. Director approval cannot cure a below-floor offer or a missing required input.

## Supported quote terms

The synthetic pricing path supports USD only, standard_upfront payment, and whole-month service without proration. Quote expiry, delivery, procurement, shipment, installation, and service-start dates must be supplied for appraisal. A changed price, scope, date, term, or cost assumption requires a new quote version and fresh approval. A new cost feed requires review before release.

## Use in sales conversations

Customer research and competitor claims can explain value but never override economic or authority gates. A customer's budget or a reported competing price is context, not approval. Only the pricing engine's calculated result and the required human approval determine whether a proposed quote can be released. Customer-facing material should contain approved terms and omit internal appraisal details.
