## Summary
Standard catalog of enterprise managed services, support tiers, and service level agreements.

## Managed Support Tiers
Tier 1 includes 24/7 monitoring, remote diagnosis, and automated patch distribution. Tier 2 adds four-hour on-site hardware replacement and dedicated technical account management.

## Service Level Agreements
Hardware uptime guarantee is 99.99% for dual-feed rack systems. On-site dispatch SLA is 4 hours for metropolitan coverage areas and next business day for remote facilities.
