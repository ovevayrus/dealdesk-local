# Loss note - Cobalt Ridge versus Vantor

## Decision snapshot

A customer chose Vantor Managed IT over Cobalt Ridge Systems because it wanted a multi-year outsourced contract. The customer regarded a longer committed service relationship as a benefit. This synthetic loss note records that preference, not a judgment that one service model fits every buyer.

## What Vantor offered

Vantor provides a full managed service and requires a 36-month minimum term. Its installation is subcontracted according to the available competitive notes. For this customer, the longer outsourcing commitment aligned with the buying plan. No deal price or detailed service comparison is established by this note.

## What Cobalt Ridge offered

Cobalt Ridge's reference service bundle covers the first 12 months of managed service with CR-200 hardware and installation. Its certified field technicians are Cobalt Ridge staff. That structure offers a first-year commitment without a 36-month lock-in. It was less persuasive to a buyer explicitly seeking a multi-year outsourced contract.

## Lesson for discovery

Lead with first-year flexibility and no 36-month lock-in when a buyer values a bounded initial commitment. Ask whether the buyer instead wants a multi-year outsourced relationship before making that point central. A preference for long-term outsourcing can outweigh service and installation differences. Do not infer a buyer's term preference from industry alone.

## Application and limits

Lakeshore requests a fixed first-year price, but its renewal intent after month 12 is unconfirmed. Confirm that intent before positioning first-year flexibility as decisive. The loss is qualitative and does not set a price or approval precedent. Any proposed discount remains subject to synthetic-demo-v1 gates and human approval.
