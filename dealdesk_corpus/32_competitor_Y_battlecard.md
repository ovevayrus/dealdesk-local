# Competitor Y — Summit Systems (battlecard)
- Product: SX-1 appliance, higher spec than EN-1 (more RAM), ~$1,800/unit list.
- Service: strong on-site presence, premium pricing (~$35/unit/month).
- Equivalent 100-unit, 12-mo scope ≈ $222,000 list. Rarely discounts below 10%.
- Wins against us on: enterprise security documentation, on-site SLA.
- Loses to us on: price, lead time (8–10 weeks).
- Talk track: when customer needs on-site SLA, position MS-PLUS add-on instead of conceding price.
