# Competitor X — Granite Edge Inc. (battlecard)
- Product: GE-200 appliance, comparable to EN-1; 2-yr warranty (vs our 3).
- Service: remote-only, no advance replacement; P1 response 4 hours.
- Typical pricing (validated, 3 deals 2026): ~$1,350/unit list, bundles 12-mo service at ~$15/unit/month. Equivalent 100-unit, 12-mo scope ≈ $153,000 list; discounts seen to ~$138,000.
- Wins against us on: headline unit price, aggressive discounting at quarter end.
- Loses to us on: install capacity, SLA, warranty length.
- Talk track: compare 12-month total cost including replacement risk; ask what happens when a unit fails in week 3.
