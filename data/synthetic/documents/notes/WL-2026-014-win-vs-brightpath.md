# Win note - Cobalt Ridge versus Brightpath

## Decision snapshot

A similar 24x7 warehouse customer chose Cobalt Ridge Systems over Brightpath Devices despite a higher price. The customer valued the ability to keep operations covered outside business hours. This is a synthetic field note about a prior decision, not a forecast for any open account.

## Why the customer chose Cobalt Ridge

Cobalt Ridge offered 24x7 remote monitoring through its managed service. The service also included a 4-hour remote response target and next-business-day advance hardware replacement. The customer identified monitoring coverage and replacement speed as the deciding factors. Cobalt Ridge staff installation gave the customer one vendor for the rollout and support.

## Brightpath comparison

Brightpath was viewed as a hardware-focused reseller with 9x5 business-hours support. Its return-to-depot replacement model had a 5-7 business-day window in the available competitive notes. The customer accepted a higher price for a service model better aligned with continuous warehouse activity. This note does not establish a verified price gap or a current Brightpath proposal.

## Application to Lakeshore

Lakeshore's six distribution centers also operate three shifts. Its stated must-haves include 24x7 monitoring and one accountable vendor for hardware, installation, and support. Use the win as a qualitative example of why full first-year scope can matter more than hardware price alone. Check Lakeshore's own priorities and delivery needs before drawing a direct comparison.
