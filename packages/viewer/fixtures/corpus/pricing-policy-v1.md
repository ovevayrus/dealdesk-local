## Summary
Corporate pricing governance, discount thresholds, and approval authorities.

## Margin Floors
The non-negotiable stress-margin floor is 25.00%. No quote may be issued below this margin floor without executive exception approval.

## Discount Policy
Account executives and sales representatives hold discretionary authority for discounts up to 10.00%. Discounts between 10.01% and 20.00% require sales director approval. Discounts exceeding 20.00% require VP of Revenue authorization.
