# Cobalt Ridge Managed Edge Service - service description

## Included service

Cobalt Ridge Systems is a fictional enterprise hardware and managed-service seller. Its Cobalt Ridge Managed Edge Service provides 24x7 remote monitoring for active CR-200 Edge Gateway units. The service includes quarterly firmware and security patching. The reference bundle includes 12 whole months of service for 100 active units beginning 2026-12-01.

## Response and replacement

The managed service has a 4-hour remote response target. Next-business-day advance hardware replacement is included. These commitments address remote triage and replacement timing; they do not state an on-site repair-time guarantee. Sales descriptions should preserve the distinction between a response target and a resolution promise.

## Installation approach

Installation is performed by Cobalt Ridge certified field technicians who are Cobalt Ridge staff, not subcontractors. ## Why bundled accountability matters

For a 24x7 site, interruptions can cross the boundary between hardware, installation, and monitoring. One Cobalt Ridge team owns those elements in the first-year bundle, giving operations a single accountable vendor. That arrangement supports clear escalation across three-shift sites without relying on an installation subcontractor. It does not remove the need to confirm the customer's site-readiness and rollout plan.
