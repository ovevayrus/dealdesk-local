## Commercial fit

The 36-month requirement may matter to buyers seeking a fixed first-year commitment without a longer lock-in. The reported first-year figure should not be treated as a validated side-by-side quote. Ask buyers whether a multi-year outsourced arrangement is a goal before using term flexibility as a differentiator. Cobalt Ridge's reference bundle covers the first 12 months of service.
