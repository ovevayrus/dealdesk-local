# Pricing and discount policy (summary — authoritative version owned by Jai, policy v1)
- Minimum gross margin on stress-case cost: 25%.
- Discount authority: rep up to 10%; sales director up to 20%; below economic floor requires finance exception (separate process).
- Nonstandard payment terms (anything beyond Net 30) require finance review.
- Quote validity default 30 days. Quotes with procurement after Dec 31, 2026 require cost-adjustment clause or re-price.
- Historical exceptions are not precedents.
